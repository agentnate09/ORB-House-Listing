About This Home

Located within the cozy neighborhood-filled city of Sigtuna stands a charming collection of townhouses within an HOA community. This property features bright natural lighting, clean modern finishes, a quiet atmosphere, and thoughtfully renovated living spaces designed for handicapped individuals all thanks to the careful ownership and maintenance of the previous owner, A Man Called Ove. Nestled amongst calm residential streets surrounded by neighboring homes, shared green spaces, and fenced off areas, the home offers a peaceful environment with a thriving neighborhood community ideal for families and anyone seeking comfort and stability. Neighborhood Details

Parvaneh

Formerly known as “The Foreign Pregnant Woman” by Ove, Parvaneh is a friendly, loving, and caring woman that helped Ove open up to others and always forced him to do the right thing. Parvaneh, along with her husband and three children, inserted herself into Ove’s life and never left until he opened up. With her help, Ove begins to treat others with more compassion and view them as more than just complete twits.

Patrick

IT consultant Patrick is known to Ove as the Lanky One and the idiot husband to Parvaneh. Patrick can be described as awkward and unknowing, failing at simple tasks and ultimately requiring much help from Ove. While he was never seen in quite the same light as his wife, Patrick was ultimately accepted by Ove despite his stupidity.

Rune

Rune was once one of Ove’s closest friends and allies. While he originally shared many of the same values of hard work and responsibility as Ove did, their relationship strained over their long years and never quite recovered.

Anita

Anita is the wife of Rune and one of the most vital pillars of this neighborhood’s community. She’s compassionate and resilient throughout any hardship, especially while fighting the city council to allow her to care for Rune while he struggles with Alzheimer’s. While initially Ove believed she wasn’t capable, he eventually learned to rely on her.

Jimmy

Jimmy is the kind and lively young overweight man who lives in the house between Rune and Ove. Despite Ove’s grumpiness, he remains happy and kind, unbothered by many of Ove’s stark remarks or comments.

Mirsad

Misrad is a young man that works in a cafe with Adrian. After coming out to his father as gay, Ove was the first to support him despite initially thinking of him as an anxious person. He later married Jimmy and adopted a daughter.

Adrian

Adrian is a teenager who works with Misrad and frequently visits Ove’s neighborhood to catch glimpses of the girl he is in love with. He’s awkward but hardworking, gaining the respect of Ove. With Ove’s help, Adrian fixes his bike, buys a proper car, and even inherits Ove’s prized Saab after his death.

Lena

Lena is a reporter for the local paper who desperately wanted to interview Ove following his “heroic” rescue of a man who fell onto train tracks. While her incessant and determined personality drove Ove crazy, her unique skills and traits eventually helped the neighborhood fight back against the “man in the white shirt” who tried to put Rune in assisted living.

Past Owner History

Before meeting Sonja, the love of his life, Ove lived a difficult and quiet life shaped entirely by his father’s influence. HIs father instilled in him discipline, honesty, responsibility, and the importance of hard work, making him the person he was. After the loss of both his parents at a young age, Ove became isolated and emotionally reserved. He created a strict routine and schedule of practical responsibilities to avoid relationships.



Ove’s life changed completely after meeting Sonja. Her warmth, optimism, and intelligence perfectly balanced Ove’s strict personality, giving them both happiness and purpose that none of them knew before. Together they built a loving life within their neighborhood home and built a lasting community with those around them, sharing routines, memories and dreams. However, many things begin to change in their lives following a bus crash that left Sonja paralyzed. To make matters worse, men in white shirts try to force Sonja into an assisted living home which Ove fights desperately to prevent. Despite the hardships, Sonja always tried her hardest to get Ove to see the compassion and beauty in the world around him, helping him be more patient and understanding. After Sonja loses her fight to cancer, her influence on Ove’s life begins to slip as he regresses back to his seemingly bitter and rude ways.



In the years following Sonja’s death, Ove slowly began reconnecting with the people around him through unexpected friendships and acts of kindness. The introduction to his new neighbors, Parvaneh and Patrick are the primary causes of this. After forcefully inserting herself into his life, Parvaneh brought connection and energy back into Ove;s life, helping him rediscover the importance of belonging to a community. Though he still remained stubborn and attached to his routines, he gradually began to accept change and appreciate those that cared about him. His fight against the men in white shirts to save his old friend Rune from assisted living homes symbolizes just how much he had grown, showing how compassion and friendship could change a person’s life, regardless if they’re stuck up in their old ways.
